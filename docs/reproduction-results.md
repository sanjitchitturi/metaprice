These metrics use interpolated product curves and per-product normalization
computed before the support/query division. Query observations affect those
transformations, so this run does not measure performance on unseen future
transactions. The revenue ratio compares predicted and observed revenue on the
constructed price grid; it is not realized revenue uplift.

## Interpretation

This run does not reproduce the paper's published MetaPrice RMSE of 0.149,
revenue ratio of 0.97, or 142 ms adaptation time. Do not use it to claim an
exact numerical reproduction. Plausible sources of divergence include missing
original experiment artifacts, the paper's internally conflicting eligibility
description, dataset-version and preprocessing choices, and hardware-dependent
timings. Establishing a numerical reproduction requires the authors' original
dataset snapshot, split manifest, preprocessing details, and training artifacts.
